## Steps
### Onion Soup (Recipe 139)
- **Ingredients**: 8 medium onions, 3 oz butter, 1 tbsp rice‑flour, salt, pepper, 1 tsp powdered sugar, butter‑flour thickener, 2 qt water.
- **Method**: Cut onions small, fry in butter until well‑done. Mix rice‑flour with water, add to onions with seasoning and sugar, simmer until tender. Thicken with butter and flour, then serve.
- **Time**: 2 hours; **Cost**: 4 d per quart; **Season**: winter; **Serves**: 8.

### Pan Kail (Cabbage Soup, Recipe 140)
- **Ingredients**: 2 lb cabbage or Savoy greens, ¼ lb butter or dripping, salt, pepper, oatmeal (thickener), 2 qt water.
- **Method**: Chop cabbage finely, thicken water with oatmeal, add cabbage and butter, season, simmer 1½ hr. Optionally blanch, mash, add liquor, thicken with bread or biscuit.
- **Time**: 1½ hr; **Cost**: 1½ d per quart; **Season**: all year, better in winter; **Serves**: 8.

### Parsnip Soup (Recipe 141)
- **Ingredients**: 1 lb sliced parsnips, 2 oz butter, salt, cayenne, 1 qt stock No. 106.
- **Method**: Melt butter, add parsnips, simmer until tender. Add ~½ pt stock, boil 30 min. Strain, return stock, season, boil, serve.
- **Time**: 2 hr; **Cost**: 6 d per quart; **Season**: Oct‑Apr; **Serves**: 4.

### Green Pea Soup (Recipe 142)
- **Ingredients**: 3 pt green peas, ¼ lb butter, 2–3 thin ham slices, 6 sliced onions, 4 shredded lettuces, crumbs of 2 French rolls, 2 handfuls spinach, 1 lump sugar, 2 qt common stock.
- **Method**: Combine butter, ham, 1 qt peas, onions, lettuces with 1 pt stock, simmer 1 hr. Add remaining stock, roll crumbs, boil another hour. Boil spinach, squeeze dry, sieve soup with spinach for colour. Add young peas, sugar, boil, season, serve.
- **Time**: 2½ hr; **Cost**: 1 s 9 d per quart; **Season**: Jun‑Aug; **Serves**: 10.

### Winter Yellow Pea Soup (Recipe 143)
- **Ingredients**: 1 qt split peas, 2 lb beef shin, meat/poultry trimmings, bacon slice, 2 large carrots, 2 turnips, 5 large onions, 1 head celery, seasoning, 2 qt soft water, bones, 2 qt stock or meat liquor.
- **Method**: Soak peas overnight, discard foam, boil in water till pulpy. Add all other ingredients, simmer 2 hr, stir occasionally. Strain, skim, season, serve with toasted bread dice.
- **Time**: 4 hr; **Cost**: 6 d per quart; **Season**: year‑round, best cold weather; **Serves**: 12.

### Inexpensive Pea Soup (Recipe 144)
- **Ingredients**: ¼ lb onions, ¼ lb carrots, 2 oz celery, ¾ lb split peas, a little mint (shredded), 1 tbsp coarse brown sugar, salt, pepper, 4 qt water or meat liquor.
- **Method**: Fry vegetables 10 min in butter/dripping, add water, bring to boil, add peas, simmer ~3 hr until peas done. Add sugar, seasoning, mint, boil ¼ hr, serve.
- **Time**: 3½ hr; **Cost**: 1½ d per quart; **Season**: winter; **Serves**: 12.

### Potato Soup I (Recipe 145)
- **Ingredients**: 4 lb mealy potatoes (dry‑boiled), pepper, salt, 2 qt stock No. 105.
- **Method**: Mash boiled potatoes smooth, gradually add boiling stock, sieve, season, simmer 5 min, skim, serve with fried bread.
- **Time**: ½ hr; **Cost**: 10 d per quart; **Season**: Sep‑Mar; **Serves**: 8.

### Potato Soup II (Recipe 146)
- **Ingredients**: 1 lb beef shin, 1 lb potatoes, 1 onion, ½ pt peas, 2 oz rice, 2 celery heads, pepper, salt, 3 qt water.
- **Method**: Slice beef, chop potatoes and onion, combine with water, peas, rice; stew till meat releases gravy, strain, return beef, pulp other ingredients through coarse sieve, return pulp, add celery, simmer till tender, season, serve with fried bread.
- **Time**: 3 hr; **Cost**: 4 d per quart; **Season**: Sep‑Mar; **Serves**: 12.

### Very Economical Potato Soup (Recipe 147)
- **Ingredients**: 4 medium potatoes, thick slice bread, 6 leeks (thinly sliced), 1 teacup rice, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, 2 qt water.
- **Method**: Bring water to full boil, add all ingredients except rice, salt, pepper; cover, bring to brisk boil, then add rice, salt, pepper; boil slowly 1 hr until done and juices extracted.
- **Time**: 2½ hr; **Cost**: 3 d per quart; **Season**: winter; **Serves**: 8.

### Prince of Wales's Soup (Recipe 148)
- **Ingredients**: 12 turnips, 1 lump sugar, 2 tbsp strong veal stock, salt, white pepper, 2 qt bright stock No. 105, small bread pieces (shillings‑size).
- **Method**: Peel turnips, cut into very small balls, cook in bright stock until tender, add veal stock and seasoning. Moisten bread pieces with stock, place in tureen, pour soup over without shaking.
- **Time**: 2 hr; **Season**: winter; **Serves**: 8.

### Spring Soup (Potage Printanier, Recipe 149)
- **Ingredients**: ½ pt green peas (in season), a little chervil, 2 shredded lettuces, 2 onions, small bunch parsley, 2 oz butter, yolks of 3 eggs, 1 pt water, seasoning, 2 qt stock No. 105.
- **Method**: Simmer chervil, lettuces, onions, parsley, butter in 1 pt water until tender, season, strain vegetables, mix two‑thirds of their cooking liquid with stock. Beat egg yolks with remaining liquid, temper over fire, then at serving combine yolk mixture and strained vegetables into soup.
- **Time**: ¾ hr; **Cost**: 1 s per quart; **Season**: May‑Oct; **Serves**: 8.

### Rice Soup I (Recipe 150)
- **Ingredients**: 4 oz Patna rice, salt, cayenne, mace, 2 qt white stock.
- **Method**: Boil rice 5 min, drain, add to boiling stock, stew until tender, season, serve quickly.
- **Time**: 1 hr; **Cost**: 1 s 3 d per quart; **Season**: year‑round; **Serves**: 8.

### Rice Soup II (Recipe 151)
- **Ingredients**: 6 oz rice, yolks of 4 eggs, ½ pt cream, >2 qt stock No. 105.
- **Method**: Boil rice in stock, rub half through a tammy, return stock to stewpan, add rice, simmer 5 min. Beat yolks with boiled cream, strain, remove soup from fire, stir in egg‑cream mixture, heat gradually without boiling.
- **Time**: 2 hr; **Cost**: 1 s 4 d per quart; **Season**: year‑round; **Serves**: 8.

### Sago Soup (Recipe 152)
- **Ingredients**: 5 oz sago, 2 qt stock No. 105.
- **Method**: Wash sago in boiling water, add gradually to boiling stock, simmer until sago dissolves into a jelly.
- **Time**: ~1 hr; **Cost**: 10 d per quart; **Season**: year‑round; **Serves**: 8.
- **Note**: Adding yolks of 2 eggs beaten with a little boiled cream at serving improves the soup.

### Semolina Soup (Recipe 153)
- **Ingredients**: 5 oz semolina, 2 qt boiling stock No. 105 or 106.
- **Method**: Drop semolina into boiling stock, stir continuously to prevent burning, simmer ½ hr, serve.
- **Time**: ½ hr; **Cost**: 10 d per quart (or 4 d); **Season**: year‑round; **Serves**: 8.

### Soup à la Solferino (Recipe 154)
- **Ingredients**: 4 eggs, ½ pt cream, 2 oz fresh butter, salt, pepper, a little flour, 2 qt bouillon No. 105.
- **Method**: Beat eggs, combine with cream, butter, seasoning; stir in enough flour to form dough‑like consistency, shape into balls, fry in butter, place in tureen, pour boiling bouillon over.
- **Time**: 1 hr; **Cost**: 1 s 3 d per quart; **Season**: year‑round; **Serves**: 8.

### French Spinach Soup (Recipe 155)
- **Ingredients**: Spinach enough to half‑fill a vegetable dish when boiled, 2 qt clear medium stock No. 105.
- **Method**: Form boiled spinach into egg‑sized balls, drop into soup tureen; the green spinach provides contrast to brown gravy.
- **Time**: 1 hr; **Cost**: 1 s per quart; **Season**: Oct‑Jun; **Serves**: 8.

### Tapioca Soup (Recipe 156)
- **Ingredients**: 5 oz tapioca, 2 qt stock No. 105 or 106.
- **Method**: Put tapioca into cold stock, bring gradually to boil, simmer gently until tender, serve.
- **Time**: >1 hr; **Cost**: 1 s or 6 d per quart; **Season**: year‑round; **Serves**: 8.

## Tips & Warnings
- For most soups, taste and adjust seasoning near the end of cooking.
- When thickening with flour or butter, add gradually to avoid lumps.
- Several recipes note that sugar can counteract bitterness (e.g., green pea soup) and that raw parsley helps neutralise onion breath.
- Some soups benefit from sieving to achieve a smooth texture (e.g., onion, parsnip, pea soups).
